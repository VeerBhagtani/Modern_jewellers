Reminders, GST, leads and business questions, handled by a model running on this page.

Arouse is a small language model trained from scratch for one job: turning what you ask into tool calls, then checking the result. It runs in your browser, so nothing you type leaves this device.

Downloading model weights

It sets and cancels reminders, saves notes, reads your files, calculates GST, finds leads in your customer list, and answers questions from its built-in knowledge base (GST, gold and jewellery, dairy, small business; GST rates as of 22 September 2025). Anything else gets an honest "I don't know that yet". It can misread unusual phrasing.

Runs on your device. Reminders fire while this page is open.
